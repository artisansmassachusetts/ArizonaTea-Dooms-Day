Why Is Arizona Tea Still 99 Cents?

Few beverage prices are as recognizable as the famous Arizona Tea 99¢ can. For decades, Arizona Tea has been associated with value, affordability, and one of the most iconic price tags in convenience-store history.

The Famous 99¢ Can

Arizona Tea became famous for printing 99¢ directly on many of its classic tall cans. The price became part of the brand's identity, turning the can itself into a cultural icon recognized across the United States.

Why People Talk About Arizona Tea Prices

In an era where the prices of many products have increased over time, Arizona Tea's connection to the 99¢ can has made it a frequent topic of discussion online. Fans often share photos and stories whenever they encounter the iconic can in stores.

A Symbol of Affordability

Whether viewed as a marketing success story or simply a beloved part of convenience-store culture, the Arizona Tea can has become a symbol of affordability that many customers instantly recognize.

Arizona Tea Price Watch

Arizona Tea Price Watch is a fan-made project celebrating the legend of the 99¢ can. Visitors can log sightings, track the famous price, and keep an eye on a clock permanently frozen at 11:59.

What Does Arizona Tea's Founder Say About Raising Prices?

Arizona Beverages co-founder Don Vultaggio has repeatedly stated that the company works hard to maintain the famous 99¢ price point that helped make Arizona Tea famous.

In interviews discussing inflation and rising business costs, Vultaggio has explained that Arizona focuses on efficiency and long-term planning rather than immediately passing additional costs on to consumers.

"We're successful. We're debt-free. We own everything. Why have people who are having a hard time paying their rent have to pay more for our drink?"

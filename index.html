Why Is Arizona Tea Still 99 Cents?

Arizona Tea became famous for printing 99¢ directly on many of its classic cans. Over time, the Arizona Tea 99¢ can became one of the most recognizable beverage prices in the United States.

While prices may vary by retailer and region, Arizona Tea remains strongly associated with affordability and value. The famous can has become part of the brand's identity and is frequently discussed by consumers online.

Arizona Tea Price Watch is a fan-made project celebrating the legendary Arizona Tea can. Visitors can log sightings, track the famous price, and learn more about the history behind one of America's most recognizable beverage brands.
